Student Complaints and Appeals Procedures Guide
Entity: Taibah University
Objective: To organize the submission of complaints and ensure fairness and transparency for students.


First: Student Rights and Duties

Rights:
- Obtain high-quality education
- Fairness in assessment
- Access to results
- Appeal against decisions
- Confidentiality of information
- Appropriate learning environment

Explanation:
These rights ensure that the student is treated fairly. If a student feels wronged (e.g., unfair grade), they have the right to object.

Duties:
- Adherence to regulations
- Attendance of lectures
- Respect for faculty members
- Commitment to academic integrity

Explanation:
If a student fails to comply with these duties, their complaint may be rejected or not accepted.


Second: Types of Complaints

- Academic: Grades, exams, course, faculty member
- Administrative: Services, registration, admission, transaction delays
- Behavioral: Violations or inappropriate conduct

Explanation:
Choosing the correct type is important because it determines the entity that will handle the complaint.


Third: Complaint Submission Channels

- Electronic system
- Email
- Student support offices
- Direct communication

Explanation:
Using the electronic system is best because it is documented and easier to follow up on.


Fourth: How to Submit a Complaint (Most Important)

Steps:

1. Submit the request
   - Log into the system or the competent authority
   - Write the complaint clearly

2. Attach evidence
   - Such as: grades, emails, photos, documents

3. Specify the type of complaint
   - Academic / Administrative / Behavioral

4. Send the request

What happens after submission?
- Request review
- Referral to the competent authority
- Case study
- Decision making
- Notification of the result

Practical example:
If your grade is unfair:
- Write: I object to my grade in subject X
- Attach evidence (such as exam paper or assessment details)
- Send the request
- It will be reviewed by the department


Fifth: Academic Appeal

Steps:
1. Submit the request within a specified period
2. Request review
3. Referral to the department
4. Study of the case
5. Issuance of the decision

Explanation:
You must submit the appeal quickly (within a specified time). The clearer the justification, the higher your chance of acceptance.


Sixth: Administrative Complaints

Steps:
1. Submit the complaint
2. Review it
3. Process it
4. Respond to you

Explanation:
Use this if the issue is with: registration, schedules, services.


Seventh: Behavioral Complaints

Steps:
1. Submit the complaint
2. Investigation
3. Decision making
4. Notification to the student

Explanation:
An investigation is conducted before a ruling, so you need clear evidence.


Eighth: Conditions for Complaint Acceptance

- Submitted within the specified time
- Clarity of the complaint
- Availability of evidence
- Adherence to regulations

Explanation:
Any complaint without evidence or that is late is often rejected.
